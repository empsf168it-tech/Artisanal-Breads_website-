Our Story

Baked Slowly. Shared Happily.

Baked Slowly.
Shared Happily.

Crumb & Craft began with a simple belief: great baking takes time. We work with carefully selected ingredients, traditional techniques, and small-batch recipes to create breads and sweets that feel homemade, memorable, and genuinely special.

From slow-fermented country sourdoughs and golden buttery croissants to delicate tarts and indulgent ganache cakes, everything we bake is made fresh daily with love, patience, and uncompromised quality.

Explore Our Bakes Visit Our Bakery

The Baking Journey

Every bake is handcrafted with care, patience, and the finest natural ingredients.

1

Heritage Milling & Sourcing

We choose quality stone-milled flour, real cultured butter, farm-fresh eggs, and seasonal fruits. Sourcing the purest ingredients creates deep, natural flavor.

2

Small-Batch Hand Crafting

Every loaf is shaped by hand, and every croissant dough is delicately folded into 72 buttery layers. Small batches preserve artisan precision and texture.

3

Patient Fermentation

Our sourdough rests through a slow 36-hour cold fermentation. This patient timeline unlocks sweet, nutty nuances and creates an airy, digestible open crumb.

4

Golden Dawn Baking

Baked before dawn on heavy stone decks with gentle steam injections. The high heat produces a blistered caramel crust and golden, flaky viennoiserie.

The Art of Real Baking

At Crumb & Craft, we never cut corners with commercial dough improvers or artificial preservatives. We celebrate real butter, slow leavening, and honest craftsmanship.

Whether it's a rustic seeded boule, a warm pain au chocolat, or a chocolate ganache tart, every item is crafted to brighten your morning table.
